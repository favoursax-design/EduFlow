About Us

Transforming School Administration Through Technology

Who We Are ?

EduFlow is a digital school management platform designed to help educational institutions manage their daily academic and administrative activities more effectively. We understand that managing a school can be demanding, especially when important information is scattered across files, notebooks and spreadsheets. Our platform provides a centralised solution that helps schools organise their operations, maintain accurate records and improve collaboration among members of the school community. We believe technology should make education administration easier, more accessible and more efficient.

Our Mission ?

To provide innovative and accessible digital solutions that simplify school administration, improve academic management and strengthen communication within educational institution

Our Vision?

To become a trusted digital solution for educational institutions by promoting efficient school management through technology and innovation

Student Management

Teacher and Staff Management

Maintain staff records, manage teacher information, assign responsibilities and organise staff activities.

Attendance Management

Parent Communication
